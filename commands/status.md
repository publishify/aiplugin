---
description: Show your Publishify reviews, free previews and recent boards
---

Call the Publishify `get_status` tool with the person's language and show, in their language:
- free previews left this month,
- reviews left on their licence (short and long),
- their recent boards: desk, mode, how many roles have read, and whether it finished.

If a board did not finish, say they can resume it with /publishify:review by giving its session id. If they have no licence, mention the pricing page from the Publishify form.
