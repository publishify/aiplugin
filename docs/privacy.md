# Privacy boundaries

This is a technical scope statement for the planned plugin, not a final legal policy or a guarantee about an AI provider's retention.

- Attach manuscripts to the chosen AI application, not a Publishify upload page.
- Parse and check the file inside that AI environment. Do not send the manuscript file or extracted full text to the Publishify MCP service.
- Tool results may contain short quotations and observations. Do not claim that the service receives no manuscript-derived text at all.
- The service processes identity/email, licence and allowance records, sessions, tasks and submitted tool results. Exact operational retention and deletion rules must be published before production use.
- A persistent server-side manuscript, note or report archive is not offered in the target architecture.
- The dashboard/report is generated in the AI environment and may itself contain quotations. Sharing it is a separate user decision.
- The AI provider's attachment processing and usage terms apply independently. Cloud-based processing can occur even though Publishify does not receive the complete file.

Never include full manuscripts, real account identifiers, tokens or recovery details in public examples, logs, issues or tests.
